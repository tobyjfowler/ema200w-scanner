Signal history & outcomes every signal in the last 5 years with forward returns

200W slope is the % change of the 200-week EMA over the prior 13 weeks; Off 52w hi is how far below the 52-week high price sits. In the 2018–2025 signal history, steep slopes (≥2.6%, shown green) and deep pullbacks (≥29% off the high, green) had much better forward returns than flat slopes (<0.6%, red) and shallow touches (<14%, red). See research/slope_report.md.

The ⇩ TV watchlist buttons download a text file: in TradingView click the name of your watchlist at the top of the watchlist panel → Upload list… and pick the file. The same list is also copied to your clipboard, so on a phone you can paste it into "Add symbol" instead.

Each row is the first signal of a cluster (touch & hold re-fires while price sits in the band; "Re-fires" counts those in the following 8 weeks). Historic signals are computed on today's index membership (survivorship bias: names that fell out of the index aren't included). "Live" signals are ones the dashboard logged as they happened — that's the clean out-of-sample record.
